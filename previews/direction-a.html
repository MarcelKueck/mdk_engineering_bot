Section 1 — Operating conditions

The standard changed on 27 August 2026

REV A
2026-08-27
Anthropic releases the Model Hardware Standard (research preview)
STANDARDS  MHS · MCP · SiLA 2 · OPC UA

The Model Hardware Standard is an open, model-agnostic specification that lets AI agents discover and safely operate lab instruments: liquid handlers, microscopes, robotic arms. Open-sourcing is planned.

MDK Engineering is independent of Anthropic. We build for the MHS era and are applying to the research preview. The standard lets an agent operate the instrument; our work is making it understand the experiment, and keeping the instrument safe while it runs. We speak the neighbouring standards too: MCP, SiLA 2, OPC UA.

Section 3 — Reference build

Handling 10 µm diamond particles, one at a time

A robotic system that handles ~10 µm diamond particles for optical quantum computing, built at the University of Oxford. At this scale adhesion beats gravity: a released particle does not fall, it sticks. Microscope optics, precision stages and learned control do the job together — the machine has to be smarter than tweezers.

t+9 h · log entry

The standard arrived on 27 August 2026.

Anthropic released the Model Hardware Standard as a research preview: an open, model-agnostic specification that lets AI agents discover and safely operate lab instruments. Liquid handlers, microscopes, robotic arms. Open-sourcing is planned.

The protocol layer is arriving. Your instrument fleet is where the work actually is: which devices can speak, which are orphaned behind a GUI, and which need a safety envelope before any agent may touch them.

t+72 h · reference build

Proof: micro-manipulation at 10 µm.

A robotic system that picks and places ~10 µm diamond particles for optical quantum computing, built at the University of Oxford. At that scale adhesion beats gravity: the machine has to understand the physics, not just the coordinates.
